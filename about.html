Who we are.

The story behind Briksafe, how we work, and why we built this service for the Gambian diaspora.

Our Story

Briksafe was started because we saw, again and again, what happens when Gambian families try to build from abroad. Money is sent home. Contractors take the deposit and disappear. Materials are bought at inflated prices by someone who pockets the difference. Walls go up crooked. Roofs leak. And the family abroad — thousands of miles away — has no idea until it's too late.

This is not an occasional bad experience. For too many people, it is the normal one. We built Briksafe to make it impossible.

Briksafe is your full-service construction partner in The Gambia. You tell us what you want to build — a house, a fence, a room extension, anything. We handle everything on the ground: sourcing materials at fair market prices, hiring and managing vetted contractors, supervising every stage of the build, and sending you photo and video proof throughout.

You do not need to know anything about construction. You do not need a trusted person on the ground. You do not need to stress about whether your money is being spent honestly. That is Briksafe's job. Our only job.

Briksafe Team
Founded in The Gambia

We are a small team based in The Gambia with deep roots in the local construction market. We have built relationships with reliable suppliers and contractors over years on the ground. Every project is handled personally — no call centres, no automation, no middlemen between you and us.

We know the challenges diaspora families face because we have lived them. Briksafe exists so that the next family doesn't have to go through what we went through.

Our Principles

What we stand for.

Six commitments that guide every decision we make and every interaction with a customer.

01
You tell us, we handle it

You don't need to know anything about materials, contractors, or construction. Describe what you want to build and Briksafe manages everything from that point forward.

02
Photo proof at every stage

Materials photographed before leaving the supplier. Foundation, walls, roof, and finishing all documented and sent to you. You see exactly what is happening from wherever you are.

03
Contractors we personally vet

We only work with contractors we have assessed ourselves. We check previous work, speak to past clients, and supervise every stage. Poor work is stopped before more money is spent.

04
Phased payments, always

You never pay for the next stage until the current stage is complete and confirmed. No full upfront payments. No releasing money before you have seen the work is done.

05
No hidden fees

Our service fee is agreed before anything starts. It is never embedded as a hidden markup in material prices. You see what materials cost, what contractors are paid, and our fee — separately.

06
Real people, real answers

Every project is handled by a real person on our team. Every WhatsApp message is answered personally. We don't use automated systems for anything that matters to your build.

Our Process

How every order works.

A clear, step-by-step account of what happens from the moment you submit a request.

1
You tell us what you want to build

Use the Start Your Project form or message us on WhatsApp. Describe what you want — a house, a fence, a renovation. No technical knowledge needed. We do not need a materials list from you; we work that out ourselves.

2
We plan and price your project

Briksafe prepares a full scope of work, materials list, and transparent cost breakdown — including our service fee. We contact you on WhatsApp within 2–3 business days to walk through the plan and agree everything before any money moves.

3
We buy materials and hire contractors

Once you approve the plan, Briksafe purchases all materials from our vetted suppliers at confirmed market prices. We hire and brief the contractors, setting clear expectations for quality and timeline before work begins.

4
We supervise and send photo proof

Briksafe conducts site visits at every key milestone — foundation, walls, roof, and finishing. You receive photos and videos at each stage. If anything is not right, we stop the work and fix it before proceeding.

5
You release payments by milestone

Each payment phase is released only after Briksafe confirms the previous stage is complete and correct. You are never pressured to pay early. The final payment is held until you are satisfied with the completed work.

6
Project complete and handed over

Final inspection, snag list resolved, and project handed over. We do a final photo and video walkthrough so you can see the finished result. Briksafe remains available on WhatsApp for any follow-up questions after completion.
